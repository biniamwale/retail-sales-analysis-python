### Next Steps to Make More Money

1. **Never run out of Laptop:** It is your biggest seller. Always reorder before stock gets low.
2. **Prepare early for 2025-11:** Stock up and prepare deliveries before this rush hits so you do not miss sales.
3. **Double down online:** Since **57.3%** of your revenue comes from the website, try offering product packages (bundles) to get buyers to spend more per checkout.
4. **Check profits before dropping slow products:** Even if an item sells less, it might have a high profit margin. Don't cut items until you verify their actual profit.
